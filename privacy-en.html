Hush Baby Cam Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-24

1. Information we collect

We do not collect personal information, and the app has no accounts. Photos, videos and any information you enter, such as your baby’s name and birthday, stay on your device and are not sent to us.

2. Advertising

The app shows ads through Google AdMob, which collects device identifiers, IP address, ad interaction and diagnostic data to serve and measure ads, and may collect information over time and across apps. This data is governed by Google’s Privacy Policy; we do not receive, store or control it.

3. Your choices

iOS: the app does not request permission to track you. Android: you can delete or reset your advertising ID in Settings > Google > Ads. Using the app offline sends no ad requests.

4. Do Not Track

The app does not respond to Do Not Track signals.

5. U.S. state privacy rights

We do not sell personal information for money. If the collection of identifiers by our ad partner is considered a “sale” or “sharing” under your state’s law, you can opt out using the choices in section 3, or contact us.

6. Children

The app is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Canada

Information collected by Google may be processed in the United States and other countries and may be accessible to authorities there. Person in charge of the protection of personal information: Privacy Officer, Daddy's Camera, jul24bnb@hotmail.com.

8. Changes

We will post any changes on this page with a new effective date.
